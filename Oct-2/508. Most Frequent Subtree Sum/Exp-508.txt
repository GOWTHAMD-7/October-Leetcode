LeetCode 508 - Most Frequent Subtree Sum

Approach:

1. Perform a post-order traversal (freq) to calculate the subtree sum rooted at each node.
2. For each node:
   - Compute its subtree sum by adding its value to the sums of its left and right subtrees.
   - Record the frequency of this sum in a hash map (map).
   - Track the maximum frequency observed so far in max.
   - Return the subtree sum to its parent node.
3. Collect all subtree sums from the hash map whose frequency matches max into a result array.

Complexity:

Time: O(n) where n is the number of nodes in the binary tree, as each node is visited once during post-order traversal, and the frequency map contains at most n entries.
Space: O(n) auxiliary space for the recursion stack and the hash map storing subtree sum frequencies.

Key Takeaway:
Post-order tree traversal naturally computes child subtree sums before computing the parent's sum, making bottom-up aggregation and frequency counting straightforward with a hash map.
