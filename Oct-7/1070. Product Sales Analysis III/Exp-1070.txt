LeetCode 1070 - Product Sales Analysis III

Approach:

1. Use the window function RANK() partitioned by product_id and ordered by year ascending to rank each product's sales records chronologically.
2. In the outer query, filter records where rn = 1 to select only sales occurring in the product's earliest (first) recorded year.
3. Select product_id, year aliased as first_year, quantity, and price.

Complexity:

Time: O(S log S) where S is the number of rows in the Sales table, due to partitioning and window sorting.
Space: O(S) temporary space for the window calculation.

Key Takeaway:
Using RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY ... ORDER BY ...) accurately identifies all records matching the minimum/first year per group, handling ties naturally without needing correlated subqueries or self-joins.
