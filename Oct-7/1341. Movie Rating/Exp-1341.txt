LeetCode 1341 - Movie Rating

Approach:

1. The problem requires finding two distinct results combined into a single column:
   - Part 1: The user who has rated the greatest number of movies. Join Users with MovieRating on user_id, group by user_id, and order by count of ratings descending, breaking ties by user name ascending. Take the top 1.
   - Part 2: The movie with the highest average rating in February 2020. Join Movies with MovieRating on movie_id, filter ratings with month(created_at) = 2 and year(created_at) = 2020, group by movie_id, and order by average rating descending, breaking ties by movie title ascending. Take the top 1.
2. Combine the two queries using UNION ALL so that duplicate names across the two parts (if any) are preserved.

Complexity:

Time: O(R log R + U + M) where R is the number of movie ratings, U is the number of users, and M is the number of movies, dominated by grouping and sorting ratings.
Space: O(R + U + M) temporary space for joins, grouping, and ordering.

Key Takeaway:
Using UNION ALL with two separate subqueries enclosed in parentheses allows finding two independent aggregates with their own ordering and limit criteria in a single unified result set.
